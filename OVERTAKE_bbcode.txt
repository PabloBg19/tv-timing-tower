[B]TV Timing Tower[/B] replaces F1 25's standings board with the timing tower from the 2026 F1 world feed. It isn't an overlay or an external app: the tower is [B]drawn by the game's own HUD[/B], so it uses the game's fonts, sits where the standings board sits, and can be moved and scaled in the game's HUD editor.

[SIZE=5][B]What it shows[/B][/SIZE]
[B]Header[/B]
[LIST]
[*]F1 logo with [I]FIA Formula 1 World Championship[/I] (or [I]SPRINT[/I])
[*][ICODE]LAP 23 / 57[/ICODE], [ICODE]FORMATION LAP[/ICODE], [ICODE]Q2 08:12[/ICODE] or [ICODE]P1 33:56[/ICODE] with the session clock
[/LIST]

[B]Every car (up to 24, My Team included)[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Position, with P1 in the red box
[*]The 2026 broadcast team icons (or the game's own badges, so logo mods show too)
[*]The three-letter code beside the data, the full surname when there is none
[*]Data column and the tyre compound letter in its colour
[/LIST]

[B]Practice[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Best lap for P1, gaps to P1 for the rest
[*]White [ICODE]P[/ICODE] box for cars in the pits
[/LIST]

[B]Qualifying and sprint qualifying[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Gaps to P1; the knockout zone shows the gap to the last car through
[*]Cars knocked out earlier leave the tower
[*]Green and purple lap flashes
[/LIST]

[B]Races, cycling like the TV[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]STARTING TYRES[/B] on the grid
[*]One-decimal intervals
[*][B]GAINED / LOST[/B] as lap 2 starts
[*]Gap to the leader on laps 5, 15, 25…
[*][B]TYRES[/B] (laps on the set) on laps 7, 17…
[*][B]PIT STOPS[/B] on laps 10, 20…
[*]At the flag: the gaps at the line and a chequered flag on each finisher
[/LIST]

[B]Race states[/B]
[LIST]
[*][ICODE]IN PIT[/ICODE] in mint
[*]Penalties as the red [B]![/B] square
[*]Fastest lap in purple
[*]Green and red triangles when places change
[*][ICODE]+1 Lap[/ICODE], [ICODE]Out[/ICODE], [ICODE]DSQ[/ICODE]
[*]Rows fade when cars swap places
[/LIST]

[B]Flags, as on the FOM world feed[/B]
[LIST]
[*]Yellow, VSC, Safety Car, [I]SAFETY CAR IN THIS LAP[/I], [I]VSC ENDING[/I], red flag and [I]TRACK CLEAR[/I] blocks with the FIA logo
[*]Under the Safety Car or a red flag the tower shows the names only
[/LIST]

Everything comes from the game: drivers, names, teams, colours, badges, order and status. It works in Grand Prix, career, My Team with 24 cars, and with team or logo mods.

[LIST]
[*]F1 25 on Steam (build 25251167) with the 2026 Season Pack

[SIZE=5][B]Installation[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST=1]
[*]Open Swofty Launcher → [B]Addons[/B].
[*]Drag [ICODE]tv-timing-tower.swoftyaddon[/ICODE] onto the page.
[*]Enable it in your profile and press [B]Play[/B].
[/LIST]

[SIZE=5][B]Settings (ADDON SETTINGS, in game or in the launcher)[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Rows: all / top 10 + me / compact
[*]Names: auto / surname / code
[*]Race gaps: auto (as on TV) / interval / leader / gained-lost / pit stops / tyres
[*]Team logos: TV icons / game badges / game white badges
[*]Tyres, tyre age, background opacity, position arrows, animations, team colour bar, highlight my car
[*]On or off per session type
[*]Show/hide key (F8). [B]Ctrl+F8[/B] runs a demo with every state; [B]Shift+F8[/B] goes to the next scenario.
[/LIST]

Size, position and opacity: the game's HUD editor (Settings → HUD → Customise).

[SIZE=5][B]Offline only[/B][/SIZE]
The addon makes [B]no network connections[/B], uses [B]no UDP telemetry[/B] and touches [B]nothing online or anti-cheat related[/B]. It reads the session from Swofty's live state, inside the game.

[SIZE=5][B]Performance[/B][/SIZE]
The tower updates 30 times a second and writes only the values that change: about 20 µs per update.

[SIZE=5][B]Known limits[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Practice and qualifying lap times are measured by the addon, because Swofty's live state doesn't provide them
[/LIST]

yet. Expect a few hundredths of difference.

[LIST]
